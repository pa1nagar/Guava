Vertisol Drip Maintenance Protocol

🔄 Bi-weekly Line Flush
Flush all laterals for 5 minutes every 2 weeks to clear sediment from Vertisol clay particles.
🧪 Acid Wash
Inject dilute phosphoric acid to dissolve CaCO₃ precipitates. Frequency depends on water hardness — determine from water chemistry test.
💧 Biofilm Chlorination
Inject 2–4 ppm free chlorine at system tail-end monthly to control biofilm buildup. Verify chlorine levels do not damage drippers.

Critical Warnings

The following practices carry documented risk of significant crop loss or tree mortality. Review before each operational phase.

⛔ Premature Cropping (Year 1)

Allowing fruit to develop in Year 1 diverts energy from root and canopy establishment. Remove all flowers in Months 8–12.

⛔ Root-Collar Waterlogging on Vertisol

Waterlogging >48 hours causes root asphyxiation and Fusarium wilt entry. Maintain drainage furrows before monsoon.

⚠ Tractor Rotovation Near Trunk

Rotovating within 1.5m of trunk severs feeder roots and creates wound entry points. Use hand-weeding within root zone.

⚠ Glyphosate Drift

Drift onto green wood causes defoliation and systemic damage. Use shielded applicators; maintain 3m buffer from canopy.

⛔ Calcium Nitrate + Phosphate/Sulphate Tank Mixing

Mixing Calcium Nitrate with phosphates or sulfates causes immediate precipitation, blocking drippers. Use separate tanks.

⚠ Unperforated Plastic Bagging

Sealed bags trap humidity, promoting Anthracnose and physiological disorders. Use only perforated LDPE or paper bags.

⚠ Insecticide Spray During Full Bloom

Broad-spectrum insecticides during anthesis kill pollinators. Suspend chemical insecticides during full bloom (7–10 days).

⛔ Chemical Deblossoming on Trees < 12 Months

Spraying Urea or NAA on trees under 12 months causes phytotoxic burn. Manual deblossoming is the recommended safe alternative.

⚠ Abrupt Re-irrigation After Drought

Resuming full irrigation immediately after Month-16 drought causes fruit splitting. Reintroduce gradually over 5–7 days at 30→60→100%.
