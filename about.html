What Smart Farmer Does

Five core capabilities to help you manage your farm with confidence.

🏡

Farm Profile

Create and manage multiple farms and plots. Record crop, variety, planting date, spacing, and plant count for each plot — no hard-coded defaults.

📋

Activity History

Log every irrigation, fertilisation, scouting, and harvest operation. Your full history is preserved with an audit trail, so nothing is lost.

🧮

Transparent Calculators

Irrigation, nutrient, and procurement calculators that show every input and formula. No black boxes — you can verify every result by hand.

📚

Trusted Knowledge

Every agronomy recommendation is sourced, reviewed, and labelled with its evidence status. Unverified content is never shown as an actionable instruction.

🤖

AI Assistance

Ask questions about your farm records and get plain-language explanations. The AI assistant is scoped to low-risk questions only and never gives chemical recommendations.

💰

Financial Summary

Track expenses, record harvests, and see your profit or loss clearly. Scenarios are always labelled distinctly from actuals — no fabricated projections.

How We Use AI — and What It Will Not Do

✅

What AI Does

  • Explains your saved activities, tasks, and financial results in plain language
  • Answers questions about your farm records using only your data
  • Cites the source and date of any knowledge it references
⛔

What AI Will Not Do

  • Recommend specific fertilizer doses, pesticide products, or tank mixtures
  • Diagnose crop diseases from photos
  • Access or reveal another farmer's data
  • Execute instructions found in uploaded documents

High-risk agronomy questions are always redirected to your local KVK, agronomist, or product label. Smart Farmer never presents unverified content as an actionable instruction.

Guava Pilot Programme

Smart Farmer began as a management tool for a single Taiwan Pink Guava orchard. That pilot helped us build and test the core activity logging, gap detection, and financial summary features.

We are now expanding the platform to support any farmer with any crop. The guava pilot continues as a reference implementation — its lessons inform our design, but the platform does not assume a single orchard, a fixed tree count, or a specific location.

If you grow guava or any other crop and want to try Smart Farmer, we welcome you to join the pilot. Your feedback directly shapes what we build next.
