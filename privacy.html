Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address. We also store the farm details you provide: crop type, number of plants, state, district, and planting date.

How we use it

Your farm details are used only to generate your personalized crop care plan. We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising.

Data storage

Your data is stored securely in Supabase (hosted on AWS). Each farmer can only access their own data.

Third-party services

We use Google Sign-In for authentication and Google Gemini to generate care plan text. No personal information is sent to Gemini — only your crop, plant count, and growth stage.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and data, email us or sign out and we will remove your record on request.

Contact

For any questions, contact us through guavaa.netlify.app.
