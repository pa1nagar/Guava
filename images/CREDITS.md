## Notes

- **CC BY-SA 4.0 (`orchard-*`)**: the adapted (cropped, resized) photo must stay under CC BY-SA 4.0 and carry this attribution. The rest of the site, its code and text are not covered by this share-alike obligation, because each photo is shown as a separate work. If the orchard photo is ever merged into a composite image, that composite must also be CC BY-SA 4.0.
- **Taiwan Pink card**: `guava-pink-*` shows a pink-fleshed guava, but the photo does not identify the variety. The card is labelled "Indicative photo".
- **Skipped**: other candidates (a crowd photo, a white-flesh cut guava, green guavas on a marble table) were not used. No AI-generated images are used.
- Visible attribution is on `privacy.html#credits` (linked from the home page footer).
- To use your own photos, see `images/own/README.md`.
